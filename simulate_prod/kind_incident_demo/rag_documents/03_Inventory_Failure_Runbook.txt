Title: Investigating Inventory Failures and Reduced Payment Traffic
Scope: LogHawk Kind demo, namespace loghawk-demo.

Symptoms:
- inventory-service and order-service report HTTP 503.
- Order logs identify inventory-service as the dependency.
- payment-service receives fewer requests.

Dependency order:
order-service calls inventory-service before payment-service.
An inventory failure prevents the payment call for that order.

Investigation:
1. Compare request and error rates across all three services.
2. Inspect order logs for the dependency field.
3. Correlate request IDs with inventory logs.
4. Compare payment traffic before, during and after the incident.
5. Check whether traffic-generator activity also changed.

Interpretation:
Reduced payment traffic does not by itself indicate a payment
failure. It may result from orders failing at the inventory step.

Evidence supporting an inventory-related hypothesis:
Inventory errors and order dependency errors occur together,
with matching request IDs and reduced downstream payment calls.

Alternative explanations:
Reduced incoming traffic, stopped traffic generation, missing
scrapes or other order-service failures can also reduce payment traffic.

Recovery verification:
Confirm inventory and order successes, then verify payment traffic
returns relative to incoming order traffic.
